Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

BRBRT is a small community project. We collect as little personal data as possible, we never sell it, and this page explains honestly what happens when you visit the site.

Who is responsible

The person responsible for your data (the "data controller") is the founder of BRBRT. Publisher details are on the Legal page. Contact: contact@brbrt.com

What data is involved

When you visit the site
Our hosting provider automatically records basic technical data (IP address, browser type, pages visited, time of visit) to keep the site running and secure. We do not use this data to identify you or build profiles.

Fonts
Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser then connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address.

The BRBRT AI chat
If you use the chat on the homepage, the messages you type are sent to an AI service provider to generate a reply (the chat runs on Claude, made by Anthropic). Please do not type your name, contact details, wallet information or anything sensitive into the chat.

When you email us
We receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. If you share your own story of harassment, we treat it with care and confidentiality: it is never published or shared without your explicit permission.

What we never do

  • We never sell, rent or trade your personal data.
  • We do not use advertising cookies or tracking tools.
  • We never ask for your wallet seed phrase or private key. Anyone who does is a scammer.

Cookies and browser storage

This site does not set advertising or tracking cookies. It may store small settings in your browser, for example whether sound is on. These settings stay on your device and you can clear them at any time in your browser settings.

Why we use this data

To keep the site working and secure (our legitimate interest), and to answer you when you choose to use the chat or send us an email (your request).

How long we keep it

We keep emails only as long as needed to answer you and delete them on request. Hosting logs and chat messages are kept according to the policies of the providers who process them.

Data outside the EU

Some providers (such as Google and Anthropic) may process data outside the European Union, including in the United States, under their own safeguards.

Blockchain transactions

Transactions on BNB Chain are public by nature and permanent. Anyone can see wallet addresses and transfers, and no one, including us, can delete them.

Third-party links

This site links to independent services (PancakeSwap, BscScan, PinkSale, GeckoTerminal, Linktree, X, Instagram). When you visit them, their own privacy policies apply.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or object to its use, by writing to contact@brbrt.com. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can file a complaint with the CNIL (cnil.fr), the French data protection authority.

Age

Information about the BRBR token is not intended for people under 18.
