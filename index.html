/ 01 — THE TRUE STORY

Why BRBRT exists

BRBRT was not born in a whitepaper. It was born from harassment, from silence, and from the place that gave its founder his strength back: the gym.

Chapter 01

Silence

It started with harassment: the kind that wears you down and makes you feel alone with it.

Chapter 02

The gym

Training became the place to rebuild. Strength first, then confidence, then mental health.

Chapter 03

The choice

Instead of staying silent, the founder chose to turn that story into a mission: speak up, protect the next generation, rebuild with dignity.

Chapter 04

BRBRT

A community, an association and a token born from that story, and now a plan to build places where others can rebuild too.

/ 04 — HOW IT'S BUILT

Three pieces. One mission.

Each piece has one job. Keeping them separate is what keeps BRBRT honest, and legal.

🤝

The Association

Carries the mission: anti-bullying awareness, mental health talks, youth and school programmes. Funded by donations and grants.

Association page ↗
🏛️

The Gym Company

Runs the gym: premises, qualified coaches, the kitchen, memberships priced in euros. Funded like a real business, through founders, partners and regulated crowdfunding.

The Token

Access and loyalty for the community: perks, passes and votes. Never a share of gym revenue, never a promise of value.

/ 05 — TOKEN

What BRBR is, and isn't

A token for belonging, not for speculation. Use official links only and verify everything on-chain.

● BRBR IS Planned utilities
  • ✓A community voice: votes on the next city, programmes and menus.
  • ✓A stake-to-train pass: lock BRBR in a contract you control, get access, unlock anytime and get every token back.
  • ✓Member perks at partner gyms and a future BRBRT Gym.
  • ✓Proof that you stand with the movement.

Each utility goes live only after legal review. Workout points earned at the gym stay in the app and are never exchanged for BRBR.
